    <h2>What I have worked on</h2>
    <p>I completed a B.Sc. in Business Administration at the University of Bayreuth and am pursuing an M.Sc. in Information Systems at Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT). Additional university studies in mathematics strengthened my quantitative foundation.</p>
    <p>My experience includes research and development at Porsche, research software at KIT in collaboration with ASML, several years as a student research assistant at Fraunhofer FIT / FIM, and product and innovation work at soffico.</p>
    <p>Across these settings, I have worked with applied AI, experimental control software, time-series forecasting, computer vision, industrial data and process automation.</p>

<section class="section about-section">

  <div class="prose about-copy">
    <h2>What I am investigating now</h2>
    <p>My current work centers on AI systems that organize and interrogate complex information. One example is a GraphRAG pipeline for collecting and structuring municipal climate-policy material, then making relationships and evidence more accessible for analysis.</p>
    <p>Alongside research, I am experimenting with the design and distribution of software products and apps. That includes the less glamorous but essential questions around user needs, positioning and whether a technically plausible idea creates real value.</p>
